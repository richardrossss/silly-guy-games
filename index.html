Silly Guy Games

Silly guy: Classic Red
Where's the camera?

Setup: camera about chest high, 5 to 7 feet from him. Use the camera view in the corner to check he's fully in frame.
Clap your hands in any game to change the silly guy. Keys: C claps, F switches front/back, P shows or hides the camera view, Esc opens this menu.
Keyboard play: Be the Silly Guy uses arrows to move, Space to puff up, J to jump, click to clap. Fly uses Space to flap, arrows to steer, Up glide, Down dive, J loop. Bubble Pop uses the mouse. Puzzle uses mouse drag. Band uses the mouse for xylophone, A and Z for drums, Space to boom, J to crash.
