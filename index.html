Silly Guy Games

Where's the camera?

Setup: camera about chest high, 5 to 7 feet from him. Use the camera view in the corner to check he's fully in frame.
Keys: F switches front/back, P shows or hides the camera view, Esc opens this menu.
Keyboard play: Fly uses Space to flap, arrows to steer, Up glide, Down dive, J loop. Bubble Pop uses the mouse. Band uses the mouse for xylophone, A and Z for drums, Space to boom, J to crash.
