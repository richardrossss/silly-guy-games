Silly Guy Town

A big beach to make your own. Put silly guys everywhere, build ramps and towers, drive cars, and go inside the beach house!

Left thumb moves. Blue button jumps. 🔨 builds: pick a thing, then tap where it goes.

Keys: WASD or arrows move, Space jumps, E gets in cars and goes in doors, B builds (click to place), 1 to 8 pick a thing, H honks. Drag to look around.
Camera: lean to turn, both hands up to go, jump to hop.
